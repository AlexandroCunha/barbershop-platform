# Photos

Put your photos in this folder. The site and the owner area read it directly.

- **Formats:** JPG, PNG or WebP.
- **Size:** up to 8 MB per photo.
- **Names:** use letters, numbers, dashes and spaces, for example `hero-shot.jpg` or `daniel.png`.
- **Shape:** landscape for the hero, portrait (4:5) for barbers.

You can also upload photos from the owner area (**Photos** tab). Uploading gives a
simple name automatically, such as `hero-shot.jpg`, and adds `-2`, `-3` if the name is taken.

After copying a photo in, open the owner area and pick it from the **Hero photo** or
**Portrait** list. Files you remove from this folder disappear from the lists.
