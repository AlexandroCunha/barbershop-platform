This policy explains how personal data is handled by the booking system used by a barbershop. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. Roles

- **Controller:** the barbershop, which decides why and how its customers' data is used.
- **Processor:** [company name], which processes the data on the barbershop's behalf to provide the system.

## 2. What we collect

When a customer books an appointment, they provide:

| Data | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Identify the appointment and address the customer by name. |
| Email | Send the booking confirmation and handle matters about the appointment. |
| Mobile phone | Let the barbershop contact the customer about the appointment (delays, changes). |
| Service, barber, date and time | Organise the barbershop's schedule. |

We also record when a booking request was made, for control and security. This system does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

## 3. Legal basis

Processing is necessary to carry out the appointment the customer requested (LGPD, art. 7, V; GDPR, art. 6(1)(b)) and for the barbershop's legitimate interest in organising its appointments (LGPD, art. 7, IX; GDPR, art. 6(1)(f)), with the safeguards described here.

## 4. Who we share data with

Data is accessible to the barbershop and to staff it authorises. We do not sell personal data. Sub-processors may include: [hosting provider], [email provider], [others]. An up-to-date list is available on request.

## 5. How long we keep data

The booking system removes a customer's name, e-mail address and phone number from an appointment [24] months after its date (the retention period is set by the barbershop's operator, and the appointment itself stays in the shop's records without personal details). Messages waiting to be sent are cleared at the same time. Other records are kept while the barbershop uses the system and for any period required by law. When a subscription ends, data can be returned and then deleted after [period], unless a legal obligation requires us to keep it.

## 6. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store passwords only as cryptographic hashes, restrict access per barbershop, and keep access logs. No system is completely immune to incidents; relevant incidents will be notified to those affected and to the authority where the law requires it.
