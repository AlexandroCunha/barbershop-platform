# Legal templates

These files are **templates to start from**. They are not legal advice and have not been reviewed by a lawyer. Before you sell the software, have a qualified lawyer adapt them to your business, your country and your customers.

| File | Who reads it | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`../../LICENSE`](../../LICENSE) | Anyone who gets a copy of the code | Ownership, restrictions and warranty for the software. |
| [`terms-of-service.md`](terms-of-service.md) | Each barbershop that subscribes | Subscription, trial, suspension, responsibilities and liability. |
| [`privacy-policy.md`](privacy-policy.md) | Barbershops and their customers | What personal data is collected and why, under LGPD (Brazil) and GDPR (EU). |

Things a lawyer will usually ask you to decide:

- the governing law and the court for disputes;
- the price, billing period and what happens to data after cancellation;
- who is the data **controller** (the barbershop) and who is the **processor** (you);
- where the servers are and which sub-processors you use (hosting, email, payments);
- the process and the time limit for answering data-subject requests.
